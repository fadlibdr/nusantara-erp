The plan came from the same read-only workflow as E1 and E2 (six readers over `form.js`, `app.js`'s `f/` route and `formPagePresenter`, the harness and PHPUnit pins, the handoff, the API and the docs; two planners with opposite priorities; a judge). Scope is OQ-1's: the page form, the `f/` route with guard, crumbs and leave guard, drafts and the 422 mapping already exist for the 35 line resources (UI-4d), so E3.1/E3.2 of the prompt have nothing to protect; the package builds what `form.js` lacks (grep 0 each: "Simpan & ajukan", a caller of `errorSummary()`, a section nav) plus the defects the readers found. Main calls, recorded as deviations where they differ from README E3:
- a real sticky footer `.form-foot` at the end of `.form-page` replaces the head-bar zone (the Save control leaves the screen once the reader scrolls to the lines — measured below); the pins that move (S3, S4, S47) are re-pointed in the same commit;
- "Simpan & ajukan" reuses the action-level confirm-resubmit loop, extracted from `runAction` into an exported helper; the route passes the submit action into `openForm`, so modal callers (prefill/endpoint/onSaved) stay byte-identical;
- the gate's ≤ 10 clicks come from two changes: one click for save-and-submit (12 → 11) and a line row that appears by itself when the last row gets its first value (11 → 10); the ledger is below;
- live totals stop at DPP (Subtotal − Diskon, the server's own formula); PPN and Total stay server-side (the rate is `vendor.is_pkp × tax.ppn_rate`, neither in the browser — ground rule 2);
- the section nav is `stepper()` and appears only when a form has ≥ 2 steps (titled sections + line tables + "Lanjutan"); 84 of 92 forms have one section;
- the >8-field layout rule ships last (20 long forms; S47's "stays a dialog" probe moves from customers, 14 fields, to a 5-field resource).
